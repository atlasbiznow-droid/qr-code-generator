Why restaurants use QR menu codes

A QR code on a table tent, window or receipt lets guests open your menu, order page or reservation form in one scan — no app, no typing. Done right, it replaces reprinting costs: put the menu on a page you control, and update prices there without touching the sign.

Restaurant QR code checklist

  • Use a static, permanent code. It encodes your URL directly, so it keeps working with no subscription.
  • Link to a mobile-first page. Your menu should load fast and read well on a phone.
  • Export SVG for print. Vector files stay sharp on table tents, window decals and large signage.
  • Add a short call to action. “Scan for the menu” beats a naked code.
  • Test-scan before printing. Check it on iOS and Android, in low light, at the final printed size.
