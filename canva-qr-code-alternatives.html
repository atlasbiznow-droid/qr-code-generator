Why look for a Canva alternative?

Canva's QR app is handy when the code lives inside a poster or flyer you are already designing. But if you just need a QR code — for a menu, a WiFi sign or a business card — Canva adds friction: you need an account, you work inside the editor, and getting a clean, print-ready vector file takes extra steps. A dedicated generator is faster and gives you a file you can hand straight to a printer.

Our picks

ABN — the fastest no-account option

Best for: a single print-ready code in under a minute

Choose the type, enter the content, upload a logo, download PNG or SVG. No login, no watermark, and the code never expires because it is static.

QRCode Monkey — the designer's alternative

Best for: EPS/PDF output and heavy styling

If your print vendor wants EPS, or you want gradients and custom shapes, QRCode Monkey is the strongest free replacement for a Canva workflow.

qr-code-generator.com — when you need dynamic codes

Free static codes are available, but the paid dynamic product (editable links, scan analytics) is a different purchase. Only choose it if post-print editing matters.

Not directly on the free plan for QR codes. A dedicated generator that exports SVG is simpler.

ABN and QRCode Monkey both work with no account.
