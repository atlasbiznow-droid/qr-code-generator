Why look past Bitly for QR codes?

Bitly is excellent at link management. Its free plan, however, allows just 2 QR codes per month with ads, and paid plans start around $10/month for 5 codes — rising steeply for higher volumes. If what you actually need is a handful of permanent, branded QR codes for print, a dedicated free generator is the better fit.

When Bitly is still the right call

If you run campaigns and need to change a destination after printing, or you need scan tracking across many codes, a paid dynamic platform is the correct tool. For static print codes, free generators do the job.
