The best free QR code generators, reviewed

1. ABN Free QR Code Generator — best for speed and permanence

Best for: small businesses that need one reliable code today

Open the page, pick a type (URL, WiFi, email, SMS, WhatsApp, phone, text or vCard), add a logo and colors, and download PNG or SVG. There is no account and no expiry — the code encodes your content directly, so it keeps working forever. The trade-off: because the codes are static, you cannot change the destination after printing (that is a feature of paid "dynamic" services).

2. QRCode Monkey — best for advanced design

Best for: designers who want maximum styling and vector export

QRCode Monkey is the strongest free alternative. It is genuinely free, requires no account, and exports PNG, SVG, PDF and EPS with custom logos, gradients and shape controls. If you need EPS or heavy design control, use it.

3. qr-code-generator.com — best if you need dynamic codes

Best for: marketers who need to change a link after printing

The free static codes work, but the real product is the paid dynamic system (scan tracking, editable destinations). It runs a 14-day trial and then requires a subscription — only worth it if you specifically need post-print editing and analytics.

4. Canva — best if you already design there

Best for: existing Canva users

Canva's QR app is convenient inside a design, but it requires an account and is tied to the editor. Downloading a clean, print-ready vector file takes extra steps compared with a dedicated generator.

5. Bitly — not recommended for free QR codes

Bitly is a link platform, and its free plan allows just 2 QR codes per month with ads. Paid plans start around $10/month for 5 codes and climb steeply. Use it only if you are already paying for link management.

Frequently asked questions

Are free QR codes really permanent?

Static codes encode the content directly, so they do not expire. Dynamic codes point to a redirect that the provider can switch off if you stop paying — check which type you are creating.

Can I add my logo to a free QR code?

Yes. ABN and QRCode Monkey both allow logo uploads on the free tier. Keep the logo small and test-scan the result before printing.

Which generator gives an SVG file for free?

ABN and QRCode Monkey both export SVG at no cost, which is what you want for signs, menus and packaging.

Do I need to sign up?

With ABN and QRCode Monkey, no. Canva and Bitly require an account.
